Privacy

What this site collects (very little) and who else your browser talks to while you're here.

Last updated November 19, 2026

The short version

No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. The only personal information I receive is what you choose to send through the contact form.

The contact form

The Say hi form on the About page asks for your name, email, a topic and a message. It's delivered by Formspree, which emails it to me. I use it only to read and reply to your message, and I don't add you to any list or share it with anyone.

Services your browser connects to

Like any website, a few outside services see standard technical details such as your IP address and browser type when your browser loads something from them:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts.
  • Last.fm powers the Now Playing window.
  • Lanyard (a Discord status service) powers the Playing / Last played row.
  • Formspree receives the contact form, only if you send it.

The Now Playing and Playing windows show my own listening and gaming activity. They don't collect anything about you.

Links to other sites

Links to TikTok, LinkedIn, Spotify, GitHub, Coursera and CompTIA take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

Questions or deletion requests

Use the Say hi form. If you've written to me before and want that message deleted, say so and I'll delete it.
