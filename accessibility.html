Accessibility

I want everyone to be able to use this site. Here's what's in place and how to tell me if something doesn't work.

Last updated November 19, 2026

What's in place

  • Photos and images have text descriptions for screen readers.
  • Everything can be used with a keyboard. On the About page, pop-up windows move focus inside when they open, and the Esc key closes them.
  • Form fields have labels.
  • If your device is set to reduce motion, the scrolling banner, blinking cursors and other animations stop.
  • Pages adapt to phones, tablets and desktops, and text can be zoomed.

Known limits

  • The Windows 98 and terminal styles use retro pixel and monospace fonts in some places, which can be harder to read at small sizes.
  • The Now Playing and Playing windows update automatically every 30 seconds.
  • Content from other services, such as TikTok, isn't something I control.

Tell me if something doesn't work

If anything on the site is hard to use with your setup, please let me know through the Say hi form. Tell me the page and what happened, and I'll work on a fix.
